the UAE's weather in a nutshell

The UAE is a desert on the Gulf, so the season is all about the heat. The ratings here use Dubai, where the winter months from November to March bring warm, comfortable, sunny weather that is the clear time to visit, and summer becomes punishingly hot and humid.

In the cool season the city, the beaches and the desert are all at their best, which is why it is the peak for tourism and big events. Summer pushes life indoors, into the malls, museums and air-conditioned attractions, though hotel prices fall sharply to compensate.

Where you go changes when you go

Dubai and the coast

Warm, sunny winters, November to March, for the beaches, the skyline and the outdoor scene. Summers are extreme.

The desert and Abu Dhabi

Desert safaris and dune drives are at their best in the cool season. Summer heat makes them all but unbearable.

The Hajar Mountains and east coast

A little cooler and good for hiking in the winter, with the east coast offering quieter beaches and diving.

Best time to visit the UAE for...

Beaches and the city

November to March for warm, sunny days on the beach and out exploring Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Desert safaris

The cool season, when dune drives and overnight desert camps are comfortable rather than scorching.

Events and shopping

Winter, when the calendar fills with festivals, sport and the big shopping events.

Value and quiet

The summer months, hot but heavily discounted, for cheap luxury hotels and empty attractions.

the UAE travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Dubai?
The cooler months from November to March, with warm, sunny days ideal for the beach, the desert and the outdoor sights. It is the peak season.
How hot is summer in the UAE?
June to September is extreme, often above 40C with high humidity, which pushes most activity indoors. Hotel prices drop to match.
When is the cheapest time to visit?
The summer months, when the heat keeps crowds away and luxury hotels discount heavily.
