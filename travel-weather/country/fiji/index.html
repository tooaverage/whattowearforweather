Fiji's weather in a nutshell

Fiji is a tropical archipelago in the South Pacific, warm year round, with a dry and a wet season and the calendar reversed from the northern hemisphere. The ratings here use Nadi, the main gateway, where the dry season from May to October brings the sunniest, most comfortable weather.

The wet season from November to April is hotter and more humid with heavier rain, and it carries the cyclone risk, highest from December to March. Even so, the rain often comes in short bursts and the water stays warm and inviting, so resorts run year round.

Where you go changes when you go

The Mamanuca and Yasawa Islands

The classic resort and beach islands, at their sunniest and calmest in the dry season, May to October.

Diving and the reefs

Year round in warm water, with the clearest visibility and calmest seas in the dry months.

Viti Levu and the main island

Greener and wetter on the windward side, with the drier western coast around Nadi the main tourist base.

Best time to visit Fiji for...

Beaches and island resorts

May to October for the sunniest, driest, calmest weather across the Mamanucas and Yasawas.

Diving and snorkelling

The dry season for the clearest water and calmest seas on the famous soft-coral reefs.

Surfing

Roughly April to October, when the southern swells bring the best waves to Fiji's breaks.

Value and quiet

The wet season, for lower prices and warm water, accepting the higher rain and cyclone risk.

Fiji travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Fiji?
The dry season, May to October, with sunny, slightly cooler weather and calm seas for the islands and diving.
When is cyclone season in Fiji?
November to April, with the highest risk from December to March. Resorts stay open, but it is worth planning with some flexibility.
Are the seasons reversed in Fiji?
Yes, being in the southern hemisphere, the cooler dry season falls in the middle of the year and the hot wet season over the new year.
