When to go to China

Go in spring, April to May, or autumn, September to October, for the mildest, driest weather. Summer is hot and humid, and winter is cold, especially in the north. Avoid the national holiday crowds in early May and October.

In short

China in a nutshell

China spans everything from frozen Harbin to tropical Hainan, but the classic route through Beijing, Xi'an and Shanghai follows a clear four-season rhythm. The ratings key off Beijing, where spring and autumn bring the most comfortable, rain-free days.

Summer is hot and humid with the heaviest rain, and winter is cold and dry in the north. The dates to dodge are the Golden Week holidays around 1 May and 1 to 7 October, when domestic travel peaks and the great sights are packed.

By region

Where you go changes when you go

01

Beijing, Xi'an and the north

Best in spring and autumn, with cold winters and hot, wet summers. The Great Wall is glorious under autumn skies.

02

Shanghai and the east

Mild and humid, loveliest in spring and autumn. Summer is sticky and brings the odd typhoon to the coast.

03

Yunnan, Sichuan and the southwest

Milder year round, with a dry, sunny stretch from autumn into spring ideal for Guilin, Chengdu and the mountains.

Questions

China travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit China?
Spring, April to May, and autumn, September to October, with mild, dry weather across most of the country. Both are comfortable and photogenic.
When should I avoid visiting China?
The Golden Week holidays in early May and the first week of October, when domestic crowds and prices surge at every major sight.
Is China too hot in summer?
Summer is hot and humid with the year's heaviest rain in much of the country, though the high southwest and the far north stay more bearable.
