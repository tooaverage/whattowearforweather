Sweden, weather and seasons

When to go to Sweden

Go in summer, June to August, for warm, near-endless days, the Stockholm archipelago and midsummer. Winter, December to March, brings the northern lights, dog-sledding and snow in Lapland, though the south stays dark and cold.

Sweden's weather in a nutshell

Sweden swings between a bright, sociable summer and a long, dark winter, and the ratings here use Stockholm. Summer is the clear peak, with warm days, white nights and the archipelago and lake country at their best, building to the midsummer celebrations.

Winter is cold and short on daylight in the south, but in the far north it becomes the season for the northern lights, the Ice Hotel and Sami culture. Spring and autumn are quiet, mild shoulders with fewer crowds and lower prices.

Where you go changes when you go

Stockholm and the south

Mild, bright summers with long evenings and lake swimming. Winters are cold, dark and atmospheric.

The archipelago and west coast

Thousands of islands at their best in summer, when the boats run and the waters warm enough to swim.

Swedish Lapland

The far north, for the northern lights and snow from December to March and the midnight sun in summer.

Best time to visit Sweden for...

Stockholm and the archipelago

Midsummer

Northern lights

Sweden travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Sweden?
Summer, June to August, for warm, long days, the archipelago and midsummer. Winter is the season for the northern lights up north.
When can you see the northern lights in Sweden?
December to March in Swedish Lapland, when the nights are long and dark and the skies often clear.
Is Stockholm worth visiting in winter?
It is cosy and festive but dark and cold, with short days. Many prefer the long, bright summer for the city and archipelago.
