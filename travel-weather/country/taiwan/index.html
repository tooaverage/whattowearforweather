When to go to Taiwan

Go in autumn, October to November, or spring, March to April, for mild, dry days and clear skies. Summer is hot, humid and typhoon-prone, and winter is mild but often wet and grey in the north.

In short

Taiwan in a nutshell

Taiwan is a mountainous subtropical island, and these ratings follow Taipei in the north. Autumn brings the best of the year, with mild, dry, clear days that are ideal for the cities, the night markets and the mountain trails, and spring is a close second.

Summer is hot and humid and falls in the typhoon season, which runs roughly June to October and peaks in late summer. Winter is mild but the north around Taipei turns wet and grey, while the south around Kenting stays warmer and sunnier.

Questions

Taiwan travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Taiwan?
Autumn, October to November, and spring, March to April, with mild, dry weather and clear skies across the island.
When is typhoon season in Taiwan?
Roughly June to October, peaking in late summer, when storms can disrupt travel, especially on the east coast.
Is winter a good time to visit Taiwan?
The north around Taipei is mild but wet and grey, while the south stays warmer and sunnier, and it is prime hot-spring season.
