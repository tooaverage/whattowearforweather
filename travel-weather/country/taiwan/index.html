When to go to Taiwan

Go in autumn, October to November, or spring, March to April, for mild, dry days and clear skies. Summer is hot, humid and typhoon-prone, and winter is mild but often wet and grey in the north.

Taiwan's weather in a nutshell

Taiwan is a mountainous subtropical island, and the ratings here use Taipei in the north. Autumn brings the best of the year, with mild, dry, clear days that are ideal for the cities, the night markets and the mountain trails, and spring is a close second.

Summer is hot and humid and falls in the typhoon season, which runs roughly June to October and peaks in late summer. Winter is mild but the north around Taipei turns wet and grey, while the south around Kenting stays warmer and sunnier.

Where you go changes when you go

Taipei and the north

Best in autumn and spring. Hot, humid summers with typhoon risk, and mild but rainy winters.

Taroko Gorge and the mountains

The high country is best in the clear, mild months of autumn and spring, with cooler air than the lowlands.

Kenting and the south

Warmer and sunnier than the north, with the best winter weather and tropical beaches.

Best time to visit Taiwan for...

Cities and night markets

October to November and March to April for mild, dry days exploring Taipei and Tainan.

Mountains and Taroko Gorge

Autumn and spring for clear skies and comfortable hiking in the high country.

Hot springs

The cooler months from autumn to early spring, when a soak in Beitou or Jiaoxi is most welcome.

Southern beaches

Winter and spring in Kenting, when the south stays warm and sunny while the north is grey.

Taiwan travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Taiwan?
Autumn, October to November, and spring, March to April, with mild, dry weather and clear skies across the island.
When is typhoon season in Taiwan?
Roughly June to October, peaking in late summer, when storms can disrupt travel, especially on the east coast.
Is winter a good time to visit Taiwan?
The north around Taipei is mild but wet and grey, while the south stays warmer and sunnier, and it is prime hot-spring season.
