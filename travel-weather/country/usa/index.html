In short

the USA in a nutshell

The USA is too big for a single season. New York stands in for the temperate Northeast in the ratings here, where spring and autumn are the gentlest times and the leaves in October are famous, but the right month depends entirely on where you are headed.

As a rule, May and September travel well almost everywhere: warm enough for the north, past the worst desert heat, and clear of the summer crowds. The two dates to plan around are the Atlantic hurricane season, which runs June to November and peaks from August to October, and the western wildfire season in late summer.

By region

Where you go changes when you go

01

Northeast and Midwest

May to October is the window, with warm summers and spectacular foliage in October. Winters are cold and snowy, especially around the Great Lakes.

02

The South and Florida

November to April is prime, with warm, dry, comfortable days. Summer is hot, humid and stormy, and the coast watches the hurricane season closely.

03

The West and the parks

June to September for Yellowstone, the Rockies and the Pacific Northwest. The desert Southwest is best in spring and autumn, avoiding the summer heat.

Go for

Best time to visit the USA for

Fall foliage

Late September into October across New England and the northern states, when the maples turn and the air is crisp.

National parks

June to September, when high-country roads and trails are open and the weather is reliably warm. Book far ahead for the marquee parks.

Beaches and the South

Winter and spring for Florida and the Gulf, before the summer heat and hurricane risk arrive.

City breaks

May, June, September and October for New York, Chicago and Boston, with mild days and long evenings.

Questions

the USA travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit the USA?
May and September are the best all-round months, comfortable across most of the country. Beyond that it depends on the region: summer for the north and the parks, winter for Florida and the desert.
When is hurricane season in the USA?
The Atlantic hurricane season runs June to November and peaks from August to October, affecting the Southeast, the Gulf coast and the Caribbean side.
When is the best time to see fall foliage?
Late September into October in New England and the northern states, a little later as you move south.
