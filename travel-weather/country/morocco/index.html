When to go to Morocco

Go in spring, March to May, or autumn, September to November, for warm, comfortable days across the cities, the desert and the mountains. Summer is fiercely hot inland, and winter is cool, with snow on the High Atlas.

Morocco's weather in a nutshell

Morocco packs deserts, mountains and two coastlines into one country, and the ratings here use Marrakech. Spring and autumn bring the sweet spot: warm, dry days that suit the medinas, the Sahara and the Atlas trails alike.

High summer is brutal in Marrakech and the desert, often well above 40C, while the Atlantic coast around Essaouira stays breezy and mild. Winter is pleasant by day in the south but genuinely cold at altitude, with snow in the mountains.

Where you go changes when you go

Marrakech and the imperial cities

Best in spring and autumn. Summer is scorching, winter mild by day and chilly at night.

The High Atlas

Spring and autumn for trekking, with snow on the peaks in winter and warm valleys in summer.

Essaouira and the Atlantic coast

Breezy and mild year round, a cool escape in summer when the interior bakes.

Best time to visit Morocco for...

Cities and medinas

March to May and September to November for warm, comfortable days in Marrakech, Fez and Chefchaouen.

The Sahara

October to April, avoiding the searing summer, for camel treks and nights under the desert sky.

Atlas trekking

April to June and September to October, when the trails are clear of snow and the valleys are green.

The coast

Summer on the Atlantic, where Essaouira stays cool and windy while the interior overheats.

Morocco travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Morocco?
Spring, March to May, and autumn, September to November, with warm, comfortable weather for the cities, desert and mountains alike.
Is Morocco too hot in summer?
Marrakech and the desert are extremely hot in July and August, often above 40C. The Atlantic coast stays breezy and far more comfortable.
When is the best time for the Sahara?
October to April, when the desert days are warm rather than scorching and the nights are cool and clear.
