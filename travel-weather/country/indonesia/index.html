In short

Indonesia in a nutshell

Indonesia straddles the equator, so it is warm all year and splits into a dry season and a wet one rather than four seasons. These ratings follow Bali, where the dry months from April to October bring the sunniest, calmest weather and the peak crowds.

The wet season from November to March is hot and humid with heavy afternoon rain, but the storms often pass quickly, prices fall and the rice terraces are at their greenest. Diving and surfing both favour the dry months.

By region

Where you go changes when you go

01

Bali and the south

Dry, sunny and busy from April to October, the prime window for beaches, surfing and temples. July and August are the peak.

02

Java and the volcanoes

Bromo and Ijen are best in the dry season for clear sunrise views and safe trails. The wet season clouds the peaks.

03

Komodo and the east

Drier and sunnier than the west. The dry season brings the calmest seas for diving and island hopping.

Go for

Best time to visit Indonesia for

Diving and snorkelling

April to October for the clearest water and calmest seas around Bali, Komodo and Raja Ampat.

Surfing

May to September for the classic dry-season swells on the Bukit Peninsula and beyond.

Volcanoes and trekking

The dry season for clear sunrises over Bromo and Ijen and firmer trails.

Value and green season

November to March brings better rates, fewer crowds and emerald rice terraces, in exchange for daily rain.

Questions

Indonesia travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Bali?
The dry season, April to October, with sunny days, calm seas and the best diving. July and August are the busiest and priciest.
Is the wet season a bad time for Bali?
From November to March the downpours are heavy but brief, prices drop and the landscapes are green.
When is the best time to dive in Indonesia?
The dry season, broadly April to October, gives the clearest water and calmest seas across most of the archipelago.
