In short

Hungary in a nutshell

Hungary sits in the heart of central Europe with a continental climate, and these ratings follow Budapest. Late spring and early autumn bring the most comfortable weather, perfect for the riverside city, the ruin bars and the grand thermal baths.

Summer is warm and lively, home to the huge Sziget festival, while winter is cold and grey but has its own charm, with steam rising off the open-air baths and a festive feel in December. Lake Balaton draws crowds in the summer heat.

Questions

Hungary travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Budapest?
Late spring and early autumn, April to June and September to October, with mild weather and fewer crowds than the summer peak.
Are the thermal baths good in winter?
Yes, the outdoor pools at Szechenyi and Gellert are wonderful in the cold, with steam rising into the frosty air.
When is the cheapest time to visit Hungary?
November to March, outside the Christmas period, when crowds thin and prices fall, with cold but cosy days.
