Egypt's weather in a nutshell

Egypt is hot and dry, so the trick is to dodge the worst of the heat. The ratings here use Cairo, where the cooler months from October to April bring pleasant, sunny weather that is ideal for sightseeing, while summer turns fierce, especially up the Nile in the south.

The classic trip combines Cairo, a Nile cruise between Luxor and Aswan, and perhaps the Red Sea, and all of it is most comfortable in winter and the shoulder months. The Red Sea resorts stay warm and sunny year round for diving and the beach.

Where you go changes when you go

Cairo and the pyramids

Best from October to April, with warm, dry, comfortable days. Summer is hot and hazy in the capital.

Luxor, Aswan and the Nile

The south is the hottest part of the country. October to April is the prime, bearable window for the temples and cruises.

The Red Sea coast

Sharm el-Sheikh and Hurghada are warm and sunny year round, with the best diving and beach weather across the seasons.

Best time to visit Egypt for...

The pyramids and the Nile

October to April for comfortable days at Giza, Luxor and Aswan and on the river cruises.

Red Sea diving

Pleasant year round, with spring and autumn offering warm water and comfortable air temperatures.

Avoiding the crowds

October, November and March, the shoulder months, balance good weather with thinner crowds than the winter peak.

Desert and oases

The cooler months, when the Western Desert and the oases are comfortable to explore.

Egypt travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Egypt?
The cooler months from October to April, with comfortable weather for the pyramids, the Nile and the temples. Winter is the peak season.
Is Egypt too hot in summer?
May to September is intensely hot, especially in the south at Luxor and Aswan. The Red Sea coast stays bearable with sea breezes.
When is the best time for a Nile cruise?
October to April, when the heat in Luxor and Aswan eases to comfortable, sunny days.
