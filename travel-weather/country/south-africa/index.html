South Africa, weather and seasons

When to go to South Africa

For Cape Town and the coast, go in summer, November to March. For safari in Kruger, the dry winter, May to September, is best for spotting wildlife. The seasons are reversed from the northern hemisphere.

South Africa's weather in a nutshell

South Africa is a tale of two trips, with the seasons flipped from the northern hemisphere. The ratings here use Cape Town, which has a Mediterranean climate: warm, dry summers from November to March are the time for the city, the beaches and the Winelands.

Safari follows the opposite calendar. The dry winter from May to September thins the bush and draws animals to the waterholes, making it the best time for wildlife in Kruger and the north. Winter in the Cape is mild but wet, and brings the whales to the southern coast.

Where you go changes when you go

Cape Town and the Cape

Mediterranean climate with warm, dry summers, November to March, ideal for Table Mountain, the beaches and the Winelands.

Kruger and the safari north

Dry winter, May to September, is the prime safari season, with sparse bush and animals gathered at the water.

The Garden Route and coast

A milder, year-round climate, lovely in spring and autumn, with whales offshore in winter and spring.

Best time to visit South Africa for...

Safari and wildlife

May to September, the dry winter, when thin bush and busy waterholes make for the best game viewing in Kruger.

Cape Town and the Winelands

November to March for warm, dry summer days on Table Mountain, the beaches and the wine estates.

Whale watching

July to November along the southern coast around Hermanus, when southern right whales calve close to shore.

Wildflowers

August and September on the West Coast, when the spring blooms carpet the Namaqualand desert.

South Africa travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit South Africa?
It depends on the trip. Cape Town and the coast are best in the summer, November to March, while safari in Kruger is best in the dry winter, May to September.
When is the best time for safari?
The dry winter, May to September, when sparse vegetation and animals gathering at waterholes make wildlife easiest to spot.
Are the seasons reversed in South Africa?
Yes. Summer runs November to March, autumn April to May, winter June to August and spring September to October.
