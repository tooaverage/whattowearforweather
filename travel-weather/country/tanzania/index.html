Tanzania's weather in a nutshell

Tanzania is one of Africa's great safari destinations, plus the spice island of Zanzibar, and the ratings here use Dar es Salaam on the coast. The dry season from June to October is the prime time, when wildlife gathers at the water and the Serengeti and Ngorongoro are at their best.

The Great Migration moves through the Serengeti year round, with the dramatic calving season on the southern plains in January and February. The long rains fall March to May, the wettest, quietest stretch, while Zanzibar's beaches are best in the dry midyear and the short dry season around December to February.

Where you go changes when you go

The Serengeti and northern parks

Best in the dry season, June to October, for game viewing, with calving on the southern plains in January and February.

Zanzibar and the coast

Tropical beaches best from June to October and December to February, with rains in April and May.

Kilimanjaro

Best climbed in the dry windows of January to mid March and June to October, for the clearest, safest conditions.

Best time to visit Tanzania for...

June to October for the dry-season game viewing, with river crossings in the north around August and September.

June to October and December to February for sunny, dry days on the spice island.

When is the best time for Zanzibar?
June to October and December to February, the dry, sunny windows, avoiding the long rains of April and May.

The dry seasons of January to mid March and June to October offer the clearest skies and safest climbing conditions.
