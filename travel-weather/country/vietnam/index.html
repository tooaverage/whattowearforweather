When to go to Vietnam

For the whole country at once, go in spring, March to April, or autumn, October to November. The north suits October to April, the centre February to August, and the south is warm and dry from December to April.

Vietnam's weather in a nutshell

Vietnam is long and narrow, so its weather splits into three. The ratings here use Hanoi in the north, where spring and autumn are the loveliest, summer is hot and wet and winter is cool and grey.

Because the regions are out of step, the safest bets for seeing the whole country are the shoulder months of spring and autumn. Pick a single region and the window widens: central Hue and Hoi An are driest from February to August, while the southern Mekong and Ho Chi Minh City have their dry season from December to April.

Where you go changes when you go

Hanoi and the north

Best in spring and autumn. Summer is hot and humid with typhoon risk on the coast, winter is cool and damp, with cold in the far north.

Hue, Hoi An and the centre

Driest and sunniest from February to August. The heaviest rain and flooding come in October and November.

Ho Chi Minh City and the south

Warm year round, with a dry season from December to April and afternoon rains from May to November.

Best time to visit Vietnam for...

The whole country

March to April and October to November, the shoulder months when no region is at its worst.

Halong Bay and the north

October to April for cooler, clearer days, avoiding the summer storms on the bay.

Beaches in the centre

February to August for Hoi An, Da Nang and Nha Trang, before the autumn rains arrive.

The Mekong Delta

December to April, the southern dry season, for easy travel and sunny days.

Vietnam travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Vietnam?
For the whole country, March to April and October to November, the shoulder months. For a single region, the windows are longer and differ north to south.
When is typhoon season in Vietnam?
Roughly July to November on the central and northern coasts, with central Vietnam most prone to flooding in October and November.
When is the best time for the south of Vietnam?
December to April, the dry season around Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta.
