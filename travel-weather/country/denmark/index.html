In short

Denmark in a nutshell

Denmark has a mild but changeable maritime climate, and Copenhagen anchors the ratings. Summer stands out, with long, light evenings, harbour baths, Tivoli Gardens and the city's famous outdoor life in full swing.

Spring and autumn are cool and quieter, good for the museums and design without the crowds, while winter is dark and often grey but leans into cosy hygge, candlelit cafes and the Christmas season at Tivoli. The weather can turn at any time, so a layer is wise.

Questions

Denmark travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Denmark?
Summer, June to August, for the longest, mildest days and Copenhagen's outdoor life at its best.
Is Copenhagen worth visiting in winter?
Yes, for cosy hygge, design museums and the Christmas season at Tivoli, though the days are short, dark and cold.
Does it rain a lot in Denmark?
It is mild and changeable rather than constantly wet, with no dry season. Summer is the brightest, driest stretch.
