When to go to Cambodia

Go in the cool, dry season, November to February, for the most comfortable weather at Angkor Wat and beyond. March to May is intensely hot, and the wet season, June to October, brings afternoon downpours and a lush, green landscape.

Cambodia's weather in a nutshell

Cambodia is hot and tropical with three seasons, and the ratings here use Siem Reap, the gateway to Angkor. The cool, dry months from November to February are the clear best time, with comfortable days for exploring the temples and the lowest humidity.

March to May is the hot season, when temperatures soar, and the wet season from June to October brings short, heavy afternoon rains that green the countryside and fill the moats at Angkor. The southern coast around Sihanoukville follows a similar pattern.

Where you go changes when you go

Siem Reap and Angkor

Best from November to February, cool and dry for the temples. April is fierce, and the wet season floods the surrounding plains.

Phnom Penh and the centre

Hot and humid, most comfortable in the cool, dry season, with the Mekong high after the summer rains.

The southern coast and islands

Sihanoukville and Koh Rong are best in the dry season, with calm seas and sunny beach days.

Best time to visit Cambodia for...

Angkor Wat

November to February for cool mornings, clear skies and the most comfortable temple-hopping.

The coast and islands

The dry season for Koh Rong and the southern beaches, with calm, clear water.

Green-season photography

The wet months, when the moats are full, the rice is vivid and the crowds thin out.

Avoiding the heat

Skip April and early May, the hottest, most draining time across the country.

What is on, and when

Cool, clear mornings make this the prime window for Angkor.

Cambodia travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Cambodia?
The cool, dry season from November to February, ideal for Angkor Wat and comfortable sightseeing across the country.
Is Angkor Wat better in the wet season?
It is greener and the moats are full, with thinner crowds and better reflections, but expect heat, humidity and afternoon downpours.
When is Cambodia too hot?
March to May, the hot season, with April the most intense before the rains break the heat in June.
