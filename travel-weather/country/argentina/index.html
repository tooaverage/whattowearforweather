In short

Argentina in a nutshell

Argentina runs from the subtropics to the edge of Antarctica, so the season depends on the region. These ratings follow Buenos Aires, where spring and autumn bring the most pleasant weather and summer is hot and humid.

Patagonia in the far south is a short summer destination, best from December to March when the winds ease and the trails open. The wine country around Mendoza peaks at the autumn harvest, and the northern deserts and falls stay warm much of the year.

Go for

Best time to visit Argentina for

Buenos Aires and tango

September to November and March to May, when the city is at its most comfortable and the jacarandas bloom in spring.

Patagonia and trekking

December to March, the southern summer, when the trails are open and the fierce winds are at their gentlest.

Wine in Mendoza

March for the grape harvest, or the warm, dry autumn that follows it.

Iguazu Falls

Comfortable much of the year, with the milder shoulder months easiest and the falls fullest after summer rains.

Questions

Argentina travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Argentina?
Spring, September to November, and autumn, March to May, for Buenos Aires and the wine country. Patagonia is best in the southern summer, December to March.
When is the best time for Patagonia?
December to March, the southern summer, when the trails are open, the days are long and the notorious winds are calmest.
Are the seasons reversed in Argentina?
Yes. With the southern hemisphere calendar, summer falls over December to February and winter over June to August.
