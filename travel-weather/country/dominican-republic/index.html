the Dominican Republic, weather and seasons

When to go to the Dominican Republic

Go from December to April, the drier, cooler season, for the best beach and resort weather in Punta Cana and beyond. The wetter months, May to November, are hotter and overlap the Caribbean hurricane season, which peaks from August to October.

the Dominican Republic's weather in a nutshell

The Dominican Republic is warm and tropical year round, and the ratings here use Santo Domingo. The drier, slightly cooler season from December to April is the prime time for the beaches and the famous resorts of Punta Cana, with sunshine and lower humidity.

From May to November it is hotter and wetter, with the Caribbean hurricane risk highest from August to October, though the rain often comes as short afternoon showers. The north coast around Puerto Plata runs a little wetter than the south and east.

Where you go changes when you go

Punta Cana and the east

The resort heartland, sunniest and driest from December to April, the peak beach season.

Santo Domingo and the south

The historic capital, comfortable in the drier months and hot and humid in summer.

Puerto Plata and the north coast

A little wetter year round, with whale watching offshore in winter and good surfing.

Best time to visit the Dominican Republic for...

Beaches and resorts

December to April for Punta Cana and the east at their sunniest, driest and calmest.

Whale watching

January to March in Samana Bay, when humpback whales come to breed offshore.

Surfing and kitesurfing

The windier winter months around Cabarete on the north coast.

Value and quiet

Late spring and early summer, before the worst of the storm season, for lower prices.

the Dominican Republic travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit the Dominican Republic?
December to April, the drier, cooler season, with sunny weather ideal for the beaches and resorts.
When is hurricane season in the Dominican Republic?
June to November, with the highest risk from August to October, mainly affecting the coasts.
When is the best time for whale watching?
January to March in Samana Bay, when thousands of humpback whales arrive to breed.
