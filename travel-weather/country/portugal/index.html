Portugal's weather in a nutshell

Portugal has one of the gentlest climates in Europe, and the ratings here use Lisbon. Spring and autumn bring warm, sunny days, golden light and far smaller crowds than the summer peak, when the Algarve beaches fill up. Rain mostly falls in winter, and even then the south stays mild.

The country is compact but varied: the Algarve in the south is the warmest and driest, Lisbon and the centre are temperate, and Porto and the green north are cooler and wetter. The Atlantic islands of Madeira and the Azores are mild year round.

Where you go changes when you go

Lisbon and the centre

Warm, sunny and temperate, at its best in spring and autumn. Summer is hot but tempered by the Atlantic breeze.

The Algarve, the south

The warmest, driest region and Portugal's beach capital. Glorious in late spring and September, packed in August.

Porto and the Douro

Cooler and greener, with a beautiful September harvest in the port-wine valleys upriver.

Best time to visit Portugal for...

Cities and culture

March to May and September to October for Lisbon and Porto, with warm days and lighter crowds.

Beaches

June and September in the Algarve, with warm seas on either side of the August peak.

Surfing

Autumn brings the biggest Atlantic swells to spots like Nazare and the western coast.

Wine country

September in the Douro Valley, around the grape harvest, with golden terraces above the river.

Douro harvest, September

The port-wine vineyards come alive for the vintage along the river valleys.

Portugal travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Portugal?
Spring, March to May, and autumn, September to October, with warm, sunny weather, cheaper rates and fewer crowds than summer.
When is the best time for the Algarve?
June and September give you warm sea and sunshine without the packed beaches and high prices of July and August.
Is Portugal warm in winter?
It is mild for Europe, especially in the south and on Madeira, but winters are changeable, with sunny spells broken by spells of grey and rain.
