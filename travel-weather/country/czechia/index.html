Czechia's weather in a nutshell

Czechia is a classic central European destination with four distinct seasons, and the ratings here use Prague. Late spring and early autumn bring the sweet spot: mild, pleasant days and far smaller crowds than the summer peak on the Charles Bridge.

Summer is warm, long and very busy, while winter is cold and grey but genuinely atmospheric, with the old town under snow and the famous Christmas markets in December. The countryside and spa towns like Cesky Krumlov follow the same rhythm.

Where you go changes when you go

The fairytale towns are loveliest in spring and autumn, and snow-dusted and quiet in winter.

Moravia and the wine country

Czechia travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Prague?
Late spring and early autumn, April to June and September to October, with mild weather and fewer crowds than the summer peak.
Is Prague worth visiting in winter?
Yes, for the Christmas markets, snow on the spires and a cosy, crowd-free old town, though days are short and cold.
When is Prague most crowded?
July and August, and around the Christmas markets in December, when the bridge and old town are at their busiest.
