Chile, weather and seasons

When to go to Chile

For Santiago and the wine country, go in spring, September to November, or autumn, March to May. Patagonia is a summer trip, December to March, and the Atacama Desert is good all year. The seasons are flipped from the northern hemisphere.

Chile's weather in a nutshell

Chile is a ribbon of a country running from desert to glaciers, so the season depends entirely on the region, and it is all reversed from the northern hemisphere. The ratings here use Santiago, where spring and autumn bring the most pleasant weather for the city and the surrounding vineyards.

Patagonia in the far south is a short summer destination, best from December to March when the winds ease and the trails open, while the Atacama in the north, the driest desert on Earth, stays clear and travellable year round. Central summers are hot and dry, winters cool.

Where you go changes when you go

Santiago and the wine valleys

Mediterranean climate, best in spring and autumn, with hot, dry summers and a golden harvest in March.

Patagonia and Torres del Paine

A summer destination, December to March, when the trails open and the fierce winds are calmest. Winter closes much of it.

The Atacama Desert

The clearest skies on Earth, travellable year round, with warm days and cold desert nights.

Best time to visit Chile for...

Patagonia trekking

December to March, the southern summer, for the W trek and Torres del Paine with the longest days.

Wine and Santiago

September to November and March to May for the city and the vineyards in spring bloom or autumn harvest.

The Atacama and stargazing

Year round, with the desert's clear skies among the best on the planet for stars.

Skiing

June to September in the Andes near Santiago, when the northern hemisphere is in summer.

Chile travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Chile?
Spring and autumn for Santiago and the wine country, summer (December to March) for Patagonia, and any time for the Atacama Desert.
When is the best time for Patagonia?
December to March, the southern summer, when the trails are open, the days are long and the notorious winds are at their calmest.
Is the Atacama Desert good year round?
Yes, the driest desert on Earth is travellable in any season, with warm days, cold nights and famously clear skies.
