When to go to Japan

Go in spring, late March to May, for cherry blossom and mild days, or in November for crisp air and red maple leaves. Avoid August to October, which is hot, wet and typhoon-prone.

In short

Japan in a nutshell

Japan is a long, thin country whose islands run nearly three thousand kilometres from the snowy north of Hokkaido to the subtropical beaches of Okinawa, so a single date can mean snowshoes in one place and swimsuits in another. What ties it together is a strong four-season rhythm: a celebrated spring, a hot and humid summer, a clear and colourful autumn, and a crisp, dry winter.

Two summer quirks shape any trip. The tsuyu plum rains sweep north through June and into July, and typhoon season builds from August, peaks in September and fades through October, mostly along the Pacific coast and the southern islands. Plan around those two and the rest of the year is remarkably kind.

By region

Where you go changes when you go

01

Hokkaido, the north

Months behind the rest of the country. It largely escapes the June rains, blooms in early May rather than April, and becomes a powder-snow paradise from December to March.

02

Tokyo and Kansai, the heart

The classic route through Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka follows the ratings on this page closely. Spring and November are glorious and crowded, winter is clear and quiet.

03

Okinawa, the south

Subtropical and warm year round, with beach season from April to October. It sits in the typhoon path from August, so aim for late spring or early summer.

Go for

Best time to visit Japan for

Cherry blossom

Late March to mid April in Tokyo and Kyoto, reaching Hokkaido in early May. It lasts only a week or two in any one place, so book months ahead.

Autumn leaves

November across most of Honshu. Kyoto temples and the mountains around Nikko are spectacular, and a touch quieter than spring.

Skiing and snow

January and February for the lightest powder in Hokkaido and the Japan Alps. The deep cold keeps the snow famously dry.

Smaller crowds

January, early February and the rainy stretch of June bring the lowest prices and thinnest crowds, in exchange for cold or damp weather.

Questions

Japan travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Japan?
Spring, from late March to May, for cherry blossom and mild weather, and November for clear skies and autumn colour. Both rate great to ideal, though they are the busiest and priciest times.
What is the cheapest time to visit Japan?
January, early February and the rainy stretch of June bring the lowest prices and thinnest crowds. The weather is cold or damp rather than extreme.
Should I avoid Japan during typhoon season?
You do not have to, but plan for disruption. Typhoons run August to October and peak in September, mainly hitting Okinawa and the Pacific coast.
