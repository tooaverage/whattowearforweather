When to go to Japan

Go in spring, late March to May, for cherry blossom and mild days, or in November for crisp air and red maple leaves. Avoid August to October, which is hot, wet and typhoon-prone.

Best

Late March to May, and November

Cherry blossom in spring, fiery maples in autumn. Mild, mostly dry, and the country at its most photogenic.

Shoulder

January, February, June, July

Cold but clear winters and warm early summers. Fewer crowds, lower prices, the odd rainy spell in June and July.

Avoid

August to October

Hot and sticky, the heaviest rain of the year, and peak typhoon season. September is the low point.

What is on, and when

🌸

Cherry blossom

Late March to mid April in Tokyo and Kyoto. Chase it north to catch a second bloom in May in Hokkaido. Book accommodation months ahead.

🍁

Autumn leaves

Koyo peaks in November across most of Honshu. Kyoto temples and the mountains around Nikko are spectacular and a touch quieter than spring.

Watch out

Typhoon season

August to October, worst in September. Build slack into travel plans on the Pacific coast and Okinawa.

Tsuyu rains

June and into July. Warm and grey with downpours. Hokkaido largely escapes it.

Summer heat

July and August are hot and very humid in the cities. Head for the mountains or the far north to cool off.

Japan travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Japan?
Spring, from late March to May, for cherry blossom and mild weather, and November for clear skies and autumn colour. Both rate great to ideal on weather, though they are also the busiest and priciest times.
What is the cheapest time to visit Japan?
January, early February and the rainy stretch of June offer the lowest prices and thinnest crowds. The weather is cold or damp rather than extreme, so it is a fair trade for the savings.
Is it worth visiting Japan in winter?
Yes. Winters are cold but mostly dry and clear, with world-class skiing in Hokkaido and the Japan Alps, hot springs, and far fewer tourists than spring.
Should I avoid Japan during typhoon season?
You do not have to avoid it, but plan for disruption. Typhoons run August to October and peak in September, mainly hitting the south and the Pacific coast. Travel insurance and a flexible itinerary help.
