Switzerland's weather in a nutshell

Switzerland really has two peak seasons, and which is best depends on what you want. The ratings here use Zurich, but the experience is all about the mountains: summer for green meadows and high trails, winter for snow.

Summer, from June to September, opens the high passes, the cable cars and the hiking, with warm valleys and cool peaks. Winter, from December to March, blankets the resorts in snow. Spring and autumn are beautiful and quiet, but the high country can be caught between seasons, with lifts and trails closed.

Where you go changes when you go

The Alps and the resorts

Summer for hiking around Zermatt, Grindelwald and the Jungfrau, winter for skiing. The shoulder months are quietest.

The lakes and cities

Geneva, Lucerne and Zurich are loveliest in summer, with lake swimming and long evenings. Spring and autumn are mild and quiet.

The valleys and vineyards

Lower and warmer, with a golden grape harvest in the southern valleys around September and October.

Best time to visit Switzerland for...

Hiking and the high passes

July and August, when the trails are clear of snow, the cable cars run and the meadows are in full flower.

Skiing

December to March across the Alpine resorts, with the deepest, most reliable snow at altitude.

Lakes and cities

June to September for warm days, lake swimming and the long light of an Alpine summer.

Quiet and value

Late spring and autumn for fewer crowds and lower prices, accepting that some high lifts and trails may be shut.

Switzerland travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Switzerland?
Summer, June to September, for hiking and lakes, or winter, December to March, for skiing. The shoulder seasons are quiet and cheaper but some lifts close.
When is the best time for hiking in the Alps?
July and August, when the snow has cleared from the high trails, the passes are open and the meadows are in bloom.
Is spring or autumn a good time to visit?
The cities and lakes are lovely and quiet, but the high mountains can be between seasons, with closed lifts and lingering snow on the trails.
