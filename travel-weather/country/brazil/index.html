Brazil's weather in a nutshell

Brazil is enormous and tropical, with seasons flipped from the northern hemisphere. The ratings here use Rio de Janeiro, where summer is hot, humid and lively, and the autumn and spring shoulders bring warm, drier days that are easier for sightseeing.

The country splits into very different climates. The northeast coast is warm and sunny much of the year, the Amazon is hot and humid with a wetter and a drier season, and the southern cities have a true four-season feel. Carnival, in February or early March, is the headline event.

Where you go changes when you go

Rio and the southeast

Hot, humid summers and warm, drier shoulder seasons. April to June and September to October are the most comfortable.

The northeast coast

Salvador and the beaches are warm and sunny much of the year, with the driest months from September to March.

The Amazon and the Pantanal

Hot and humid year round. The drier months, broadly June to October, make for easier travel and the best wildlife viewing.

Best time to visit Brazil for...

Rio and the beaches

December to March for hot summer beach days and Carnival, or the milder shoulders for easier sightseeing.

Carnival

February or early March, when Rio and Salvador host the world's most famous street party.

The Amazon and Pantanal

June to October, the drier season, for wildlife spotting and more comfortable jungle travel.

Iguazu Falls

Pleasant year round, with the falls fullest after the summer rains and the shoulder months most comfortable.

Brazil travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Brazil?
For Rio and the south, the milder shoulder months of April to June and September to October. Summer, December to March, is hot, humid and the season of Carnival.
When is Carnival in Brazil?
In February or early March, the date moving with Lent. It is the busiest and most expensive time, especially in Rio and Salvador.
When is the best time for the Amazon?
The drier season, broadly June to October, for easier travel and the best wildlife viewing, though the rainforest is warm and humid all year.
