Cuba, weather and seasons

When to go to Cuba

Go in the dry season, November to April, for warm, sunny days in Havana and on the beaches. The wet season, May to October, is hotter and more humid, and overlaps the Caribbean hurricane season, which peaks from August to October.

Cuba's weather in a nutshell

Cuba is warm and tropical with a dry and a wet season, and the ratings here use Havana. The dry months from November to April are the clear best time, with sunny, comfortable days for the old city, the classic cars and the white-sand beaches.

The wet season from May to October is hotter and more humid, with afternoon showers and the main hurricane risk, which is highest from August to October. Even then, mornings are often clear and prices are lower, but the storm season is worth planning around.

Where you go changes when you go

Havana and the west

Best in the dry season for the old city and the tobacco country of Vinales. Hot and humid in summer.

Varadero and the beaches

The famous beaches are sunniest and calmest from November to April, the prime resort season.

Trinidad and the centre

Colonial towns and the Escambray hills, comfortable in the dry months and steamy in summer.

Best time to visit Cuba for...

Havana and the cities

November to April for warm, dry, comfortable days exploring the old town and the classic-car streets.

Beaches

December to April for Varadero, Cayo Coco and the keys at their sunniest and calmest.

Diving and snorkelling

The dry season for the clearest water around the reefs and the Bay of Pigs.

Value and quiet

May and June, before the worst of the storms, for lower prices and greener landscapes.

Cuba travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Cuba?
The dry season, November to April, with warm, sunny weather for Havana and the beaches. It is the peak season for good reason.
When is hurricane season in Cuba?
June to November, with the highest risk from August to October. Storms can affect the whole island, so plan with some flexibility.
Is summer a bad time to visit Cuba?
It is hotter, more humid and carries storm risk, but mornings are often clear and prices are lower outside the peak.
