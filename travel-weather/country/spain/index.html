Spain's weather in a nutshell

Spain saves its best for spring and autumn. The ratings here use Madrid, high on the central plateau, where July and August can be fierce and dry while April to June and September to October are warm and easygoing. The festival calendar, from Holy Week to the spring fairs, peaks in spring.

Climates vary widely: the Mediterranean coast and Barcelona are milder, the south around Seville is the hottest, and the green north stays cooler and wetter. Winter is gentle along the coast and in the south, which is why the Costa del Sol and the Canary Islands draw sun-seekers year round.

Where you go changes when you go

Madrid and the centre

Hot, dry summers and cold, clear winters on the high plateau. Spring and autumn are the sweet spot.

Barcelona and the coast

Milder and made for the shoulder seasons of April to June and September to October, mixing city and beach.

Seville and the south

The hottest corner of the country, glorious in spring and autumn and best avoided at the height of summer.

Best time to visit Spain for...

Cities and culture

April to June and September to October for Madrid, Barcelona and Seville, with warm days and manageable crowds.

Beaches

June and September along the Mediterranean, with warm seas before and after the August peak.

Festivals

Spring for Holy Week processions and Seville's April Fair, two of the country's great spectacles.

Winter sun

The Canary Islands and the southern coast stay mild through winter, a warm escape from northern Europe.

Spain travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Spain?
April to June and September to October, when the weather is warm and comfortable and the crowds are lighter than the July to August peak.
Is Spain too hot in summer?
July and August are very hot inland, especially in Madrid and Seville. The coast is more bearable, but spring and autumn are easier across the country.
When is the cheapest time to visit Spain?
November, January and February, outside the holidays, when crowds thin and prices fall, with mild weather on the coast.
