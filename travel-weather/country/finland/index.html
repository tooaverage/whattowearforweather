Finland's weather in a nutshell

Finland is a land of forests and lakes with two very different seasons, and the ratings here use Helsinki. Summer brings warm, bright days, the lake cabins, the archipelago and near round-the-clock light in the north, the time for hiking, swimming and the sauna.

Winter is long, dark and snowy, but in Lapland it becomes a wonderland of northern lights, reindeer, husky sleds and Santa's official home. Spring and autumn are short, quiet shoulders, with autumn's ruska colour lighting up the northern forests.

Where you go changes when you go

Helsinki and the south

Mild, bright summers with archipelago boat trips, cold and dark winters with a cosy, design-led city.

The Lakeland

Thousands of lakes and forest cabins at their best in the warm, light days of summer.

Lapland

The Arctic north, for northern lights, husky safaris and Santa from December to March, and the midnight sun in summer.

Finland travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Finland?
Summer, June to August, for the midnight sun and lakes, or winter, December to March, for the northern lights and Lapland.
When can you see the northern lights in Finland?
From roughly September to March in Lapland, with the snowy depths of winter the classic time on clear, dark nights.
When is the best time to visit Santa in Lapland?
December, around Christmas, when Rovaniemi and the Arctic north are at their snowy, festive best.
