Malaysia, weather and seasons

When to go to Malaysia

Malaysia is warm and humid all year. The west coast and islands (Langkawi, Penang) are driest from December to March, while the east coast (the Perhentians, Tioman) is best from March to October and closes for the monsoon in winter.

Malaysia's weather in a nutshell

Malaysia sits on the equator, so it is hot and humid year round with rain possible any day, and the trick is matching the coast to the season. The ratings here use Kuala Lumpur, which is steamy throughout but workable any month.

The two coasts run on opposite calendars. The west, including Langkawi and Penang, is driest from December to March. The east, including the Perhentian Islands and Tioman, is best from March to October and largely shuts down during the northeast monsoon from November to February.

Where you go changes when you go

Kuala Lumpur and the centre

Hot and humid year round, with short, intense downpours and world-class indoor attractions for the rain.

Langkawi, Penang and the west coast

Driest and best from December to March, the prime window for the western islands and beaches.

The Perhentians and the east coast

Best from March to October for diving and beaches. The monsoon closes many island resorts from November to February.

Best time to visit Malaysia for...

West coast islands

December to March for Langkawi and Penang, the driest, sunniest stretch on that side.

East coast diving

March to October for the Perhentians and Tioman, with calm, clear water for snorkelling and diving.

Cities and food

Year round in Kuala Lumpur and Penang, where the rain comes in bursts and the eating never stops.

Borneo and the rainforest

Drier months from March to October for Sabah, orangutans and Mount Kinabalu.

Malaysia travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Malaysia?
It depends on the coast. The west and its islands are driest from December to March, while the east coast is best from March to October.
When is the monsoon in Malaysia?
The northeast monsoon hits the east coast from November to February, closing many island resorts, while bringing the west coast its drier season.
Does it rain all the time in Malaysia?
Rain is frequent year round but usually short and heavy, and the cities are built around it, so it rarely ruins a trip for long.
