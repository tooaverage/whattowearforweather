When to go to Australia

In short

Australia in a nutshell

Australia is a continent, so the answer changes with where you go. The month ratings track Sydney, where the gentlest weather comes in spring and autumn, summer is warm and humid, and winter stays mild.

The far north works the other way around. Cairns, Darwin and the tropics have a dry season from May to October that is the time to go, and a hot, stormy wet season from November to April that brings cyclone risk to the coast.

By region

Where you go changes when you go

01

Sydney and the southeast

Temperate and at its best in spring and autumn. Summer is warm for the beaches, winter is cool but rarely cold.

02

The tropical north

Cairns and Darwin are made for the dry season, May to October. The wet season from November brings heat, storms and cyclone risk.

03

The Outback and Red Centre

Uluru and the desert are best from April to September, when the days are warm rather than scorching and the nights are cold.

The Great Barrier Reef

June to October, the dry season, with clear water, calm seas and lower humidity. It also sits outside the worst of the stinger season.

Sydney and the beaches

December to February for warm summer days, or March to May for swimming with thinner crowds.

Uluru and the Outback

April to September, when the desert heat eases to comfortable days and crisp, cold nights.

Wine and the cities

March to May, when Melbourne, Adelaide and the wine regions glow in the autumn harvest.

Questions

Australia travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Australia?
Spring, September to November, and autumn, March to May, for Sydney and the south. The tropical north is best in its dry season, May to October.
Are the seasons reversed in Australia?
Yes, they run opposite the northern hemisphere, so December to February is high summer and June to August is winter.
When is the best time for the Great Barrier Reef?
June to October, the northern dry season, with the clearest, calmest water and the lowest humidity.
