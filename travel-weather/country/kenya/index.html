Kenya's weather in a nutshell

Kenya is a year-round safari country near the equator, with seasons set by rain rather than temperature, and the ratings here use Nairobi. The dry months, from June to October and again in January and February, concentrate wildlife around the water and make for the best game viewing.

The headline event is the Great Migration, when vast wildebeest herds cross from the Serengeti into the Masai Mara from roughly July to October. The long rains in March to May green the land but soften the roads, and the coast around Mombasa and Diani runs warm and humid year round.

Where you go changes when you go

The Masai Mara and the safari parks

Best in the dry seasons, June to October and January to February, with the Migration in the Mara from July to October.

Nairobi and the highlands

Mild year round thanks to the altitude, with clearer, drier spells in the dry seasons.

Mombasa and the coast

Warm and humid year round, sunniest and driest from roughly December to March and again midyear.

Best time to visit Kenya for...

The Great Migration

July to October in the Masai Mara, when the wildebeest herds and dramatic river crossings arrive.

General safari

The dry seasons, June to October and January to February, for the easiest game viewing across the parks.

Beaches

December to March on the coast around Diani and Mombasa, the sunniest, driest stretch.

Birding and green season

The wet months, when migratory birds arrive and the landscapes are lush, in exchange for muddier roads.

Kenya travel FAQ

The dry seasons, June to October and January to February, for the best safari game viewing, with the Great Migration in the Mara from July to October.
When is the Great Migration in Kenya?
Roughly July to October, when the wildebeest herds cross from the Serengeti into the Masai Mara, including the famous river crossings.

The long rains fall March to May and shorter rains around November, greening the land but softening the safari roads.
