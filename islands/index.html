Pacific islands
Every island country and major territory you can fly to, and the months worth flying for.

Polynesia

East side of the Pacific. Lagoon islands, Polynesian culture, dry season May to October almost everywhere.

🇹🇻Tuvalu
One runway, one huge lagoon, almost no tourists.
June to Septemberbest time🇼🇸Samoa
Waterfalls, beach fales, and deep Polynesian culture.
June to Septemberbest time🇹🇴Tonga
Swim with humpback whales, July to October.
June to Septemberbest time🇵🇫French Polynesia
Tahiti, Moorea, Bora Bora. Overwater everything.
June to Septemberbest time🇨🇰Cook Islands
Lagoon life with New Zealand-easy logistics.
June to Octoberbest time🇳🇺Niue
One raised coral rock. Whales off the reef, no crowds.

Melanesia

Southwest Pacific. Bigger, wilder islands: volcanoes, jungles, diving. Dry season May to October.

🇫🇯Fiji
The Pacific hub. 300+ islands and the easiest logistics in the region.
June to Septemberbest time🇻🇺Vanuatu
Active volcanoes, blue holes, kastom villages.
June to Septemberbest time🇳🇨New Caledonia
French food beside the world's largest lagoon.
September to Novemberbest time🇸🇧Solomon Islands
WWII wrecks and reefs almost nobody dives.
June to Septemberbest time🇵🇬Papua New Guinea
Highlands culture, birds of paradise, serious diving. The wild one.
June to Octoberbest time

Micronesia

North of the equator, so the seasons flip: most of these are driest December to April.

🇵🇼Palau
The Rock Islands. The best diving in Micronesia.
February to Aprilbest time🇫🇲Micronesia (FSM)
Nan Madol's ruined city and Chuuk's wreck lagoon.
January to Marchbest time🇲🇭Marshall Islands
Remote atolls, WWII lagoons, and very few visitors.
January to Marchbest time🇰🇮Kiribati
Atolls straddling the equator and the date line.
June to Octoberbest time🇳🇷Nauru
The world's least-visited country. 21 square kilometres of it.
June to Septemberbest time🇬🇺Guam
US territory, big beaches, easy flights from Asia.
January to Aprilbest time

Also out there

🇦🇸 American Samoa Pago Pago. Same seasons as Samoa, US territory. Fly from Apia or Honolulu.
🇹🇰 Tokelau No airport. A ~26-hour boat from Apia, a few sailings a month. The hard one.
🇼🇫 Wallis and Futuna French territory between Fiji and Samoa. Fly via Nouméa or Fiji.
🇵🇳 Pitcairn Islands Population ~40. Ship from Mangareva, French Polynesia, a few times a year.
🇳🇫 Norfolk Island Australian territory, subtropical. Fly from Sydney or Brisbane.
🇺🇸 Hawaii The easy on-ramp: direct from Vancouver. Driest April to October.
🇨🇱 Rapa Nui (Easter Island) The moai. Fly from Santiago, sometimes Tahiti. Driest in the austral winter.

Rule of thumb: south of the equator (Polynesia, Melanesia) the dry season is May to October. North of it (Micronesia) it flips to roughly December to April. On the equator itself there are no cyclones at all.
