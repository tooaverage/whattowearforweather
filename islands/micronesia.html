GoFineWet season

There is no true dry season: Pohnpei is one of the wettest places on Earth (its mountains catch over 10,000 mm a year). January to March is the least wet and the trade winds blow, so aim there and pack for rain anyway.

Seasons

☀️DryJanuary to March, relatively speaking. Showers still come most days, usually short and heavy.
🌧️WetApril to December. The interior rainforest is drenched; coasts are milder.
🌀StormsTyphoons form in these waters but usually track west before strengthening. Direct hits are uncommon.
🌊Sea29 to 30°C year-round.

Pack

Quick-dry everything and a proper rain shell.

Reef shoes and snorkel gear.

Sturdy sandals for the muddy walk around Nan Madol.

Insect repellent.

Sakau (kava) etiquette: accept the cup with both hands.

Getting there

United's island hopper (Honolulu to Guam) calls at Pohnpei and Chuuk a few times a week. Book early; it is the only game in town.
