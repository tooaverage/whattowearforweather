Boat upholstery in Deep Cove and Indian Arm

Deep Cove boats live outside all year, and the vinyl shows it. Winter rain gets under the covers, summer UV chalks the tops, and the foam under the helm seat soaks up both. If your seats crack when you sit down in April, that's this coast doing what it does.

We serve Deep Cove Marina, the yacht club docks and boats on buoys up Indian Arm. For buoy boats we'll arrange a dock meet, since carrying a bench seat down a swim grid is nobody's idea of a good morning. We unbolt the seats, take them to the shop, and reinstall them when they're done. Your boat never leaves the Cove.

Typical jobs out here: bowrider back-to-backs with split seams, sun pads gone flat, helm seats where the plywood base finally let go. Send one wide photo and one close-up and we'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
