Boat upholstery in Coal Harbour

Coal Harbour is gated docks, paid parking and downtown traffic, which is why getting any tradesperson to your slip usually means booking weeks out for a look-around visit before anyone talks numbers. Photos skip all of that. Send two, get an estimate by email, and the only visit is the one where we actually take your seats away.

We serve the Coal Harbour marinas on the downtown side of Burrard Inlet. Tell us your dock and gate arrangements in the request and we'll sort access when we book the pickup. Seats come off at the slip, cushions get bagged, and everything comes back reinstalled.

Typical jobs here: sun pads and cockpit cushions cooked by a decade of afternoon sun off the glass towers, flybridge helm chairs, interior settees on boats that get lived in more than driven. Send one wide photo and one close-up and we'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
