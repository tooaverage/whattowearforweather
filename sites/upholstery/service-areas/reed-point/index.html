Burrard Boat Seats / Service areas / Reed Point

Boat upholstery at Reed Point Marina, Port Moody

Reed Point sits at the east end of Burrard Inlet and it's one of the biggest marinas in the Lower Mainland, with water slips, dry storage and a working boatyard. Whether your boat is in the water or up in the racks, we can get to your seats.

For dry-stored boats, redoing upholstery while the boat sits is the easy win: the seats come off, the boat doesn't move, and everything's back on before your next launch. For boats in the water we unbolt at the slip like anywhere else. Either way the estimate happens by email first, from two photos, so there's no visit just to get a number.

Typical jobs here: pontoon and bowrider benches with UV-split tops, back-to-backs on older Bayliners and Campions, engine box cushions flattened into pancakes. Send one wide photo and one close-up and we'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
