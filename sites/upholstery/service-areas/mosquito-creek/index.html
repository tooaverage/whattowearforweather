Boat upholstery at Mosquito Creek Marina

Mosquito Creek is one of the biggest marinas on the North Shore, minutes from our base in North Vancouver, and it holds everything from liveaboards to weekend bowriders. A lot of those boats are older than their owners' cars, and the upholstery is usually the first thing that gives it away.

We come to your slip, unbolt the seats or collect the cushions, and bring them back finished. No trailering, no hauling seats through the parking lot in your hatchback. For liveaboards we can work cushion by cushion so you're never sleeping on plywood.

Typical jobs here: settee and V-berth cushions with tired foam, cockpit benches with seams gone, helm chairs worn through on the port side where forty years of drivers sat. Send one wide photo and one close-up and we'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
