Questions we get

How much does boat upholstery cost in Vancouver?

It depends on the vinyl grade, whether the foam and plywood bases need replacing, and how many hours of stitching the job takes. A split seam and a full interior are very different jobs. Send two photos and we'll email you an estimate. The factors are explained on our cost page.

Do you really come to the marina?

Yes. We unbolt the seats at your slip, take them away, and reinstall them when they're done. The boat doesn't move.

What photos should I send?

One wide shot of the whole seat or cockpit, one close-up of the worst damage. If the foam feels soft or the base flexes when you sit down, mention it.

Do you use marine vinyl?

Yes. Marine-grade vinyl is UV and mildew rated, and it's the only thing that lasts outdoors here. Waterlogged foam gets replaced, not reused.

Can you fix just a seam or a small tear?

Yes. Restitching a seam is often the cheapest way to buy a seat a few more seasons.

When can you take my seats?

We're taking bookings for the 2026/27 season now. Send your request and we'll reply within 24 hours.
