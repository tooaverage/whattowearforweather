Deep Cove Mobile Marine / Service areas / Mosquito Creek

Mobile marine mechanic at Mosquito Creek Marina

Mosquito Creek is one of the biggest marinas on the North Shore, and its mix runs from liveaboards to weekend runabouts to boats whose engines haven't turned over since the last Canucks playoff run. All of them winter in the water, and all of them need the same thing before the first freeze: antifreeze through the cooling side and a fall oil change.

We come to your slip. Winterization, spring recommissioning, oil and impeller changes, battery checks, and dockside diagnostics when the engine cranks but won't fire. For liveaboards we're careful around your home: no oil in the bilge, no parts spread across your cockpit for a week.

Tell us the engine make and year, what you need, and add a photo of the engine bay. We'll email you an estimate within 24 hours, and there's no visit just to get a number.
