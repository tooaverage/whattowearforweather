Mobile marine mechanic in Deep Cove and Indian Arm

Deep Cove is our home water. Boats here winter in their slips and on buoys up Indian Arm, which is exactly how it should be, as long as somebody puts antifreeze through the engine before the first cold snap rolls down the Arm.

We work at Deep Cove Marina, the yacht club docks and on buoy boats, coming alongside when the weather allows or meeting you at a dock when it doesn't. Winterization, oil and impeller changes, batteries, and the classic Deep Cove call: the boat that started fine all summer and won't turn over in March.

No haul-out, no trailering over the Ironworkers at 8 am. Tell us the engine make and year, what you need, and add a photo of the engine bay. We'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
