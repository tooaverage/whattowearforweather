Deep Cove Mobile Marine / Service areas / Reed Point

Mobile marine mechanic at Reed Point Marina, Port Moody

Reed Point sits at the east end of Burrard Inlet and it's one of the biggest marinas in the Lower Mainland, with water slips, dry storage racks and a boatyard on site. In-water boats here need full winterization before the first freeze, and dry-stored boats still need fuel stabilizer, a fall oil change and gear oil checked before they sit for five months.

We service boats in the slips and, where the marina's rules allow, boats in dry storage. Winterization, spring recommissioning, oil, impellers and filters, batteries, and won't-start calls when a boat comes down off the rack and refuses to cooperate.

Port Moody and the east inlet are a long tow from anywhere, which is the point of a mobile mechanic. Tell us the engine make and year, what you need, and add a photo of the engine bay. We'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
