Mobile marine mechanic in Coal Harbour

Coal Harbour boats winter in the water with shore power a cable away, which makes owners relaxed about freezing right up until a cold snap outlasts a block heater. The engine still needs proper winterizing: antifreeze through the raw-water side, fall oil change, drives and batteries checked.

Gated docks and downtown parking make trades visits a pain here, so we quote by email first. Tell us the engine make and year, add a photo of the engine bay, and the only visit is the one where the work happens. Include your dock and gate arrangements in the request and we'll sort access when we book.

Typical calls in the harbour: winterization on sterndrives and small inboards, oil and impeller service, batteries that died over winter, and pre-purchase checks on boats being sold off the dock. We'll email you an estimate within 24 hours.
