Wintering in the water is normal here. One cold snap isn't.

Most Burrard Inlet boats stay in the water all winter, and most winters that's fine right up until the week it isn't.

Raw-water cooling only has to freeze once, and a cracked block or manifold costs more than every winterization you'll ever buy. September and October slots go first. What a proper winterization includes is on our winterization page.

Questions we get

How much does boat winterization cost in Vancouver?

It depends on the engine. An outboard flush and stabilize is a smaller job than winterizing a twin sterndrive with onboard water systems. Tell us the make, year and drive type and we'll email you an estimate. What's included is on our winterization page.

Can you winterize my boat in the water?

Yes, that's the point. In-water wintering is the default around Burrard Inlet, and everything we do happens at the dock.

Do boats in Vancouver even need winterizing?

Mild winters here still throw a cold snap most years, and raw-water cooling only has to freeze once. If the boat stays in the water without heat, the engine needs antifreeze through it.

My engine won't start. Will you come look at it?

Yes. Dockside diagnostics are a normal call for us. We check fuel, spark, batteries and cables before anything gets condemned.

What should I send for an estimate?

Engine make, model year, outboard or sterndrive, and what you need done. A photo of the engine bay helps a lot.
